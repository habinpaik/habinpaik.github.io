세종시장 선거

“조치원 개발” “청년 일자리 확대” 세종캠, 혜택 얻을까

Pledge analysis ↗

For the 2026 local elections I desked three races around our two campuses: an interview with the Seongbuk district head candidates, who met eight years ago and now face off again over how fast — and for whom — Seongbuk redevelops; a pledge analysis of the Dongdaemun rematch, undergrounding the railway against a Cheongnyangni economic hub; and an interview with the Sejong mayoral candidates on what Jochiwon development and jobs for young people would mean for the Sejong campus.

In each, the candidates’ answers were set side by side and read for one question: what their plans would change for the students who live, study and work around our campuses — rents, commutes and jobs after graduation.

Where a pledge came with no budget or timeline, the piece said so rather than leaving readers to guess.
